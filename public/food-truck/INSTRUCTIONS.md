# Greenway Street Eats — asset handoff

Stakeholder note: **poster trim is 18×24**. Bleed files are larger so the printer can cut cleanly to that size.

Pick **one** truck fix (V1, V2, or V3). Use that same version for Captivate, email, and poster so everything matches.

---

## What each file is for

### Captivate (elevator screens)
- Files: `V#/GreenwayStreetEats_Captivate_1024x680_V#.png`
- Use: upload to Captivate / elevator digital screens
- Do not print these as the poster

### Email (tenants)
- Files: `V#/GreenwayStreetEats_Email_8.5x11_V#.pdf`
- Size: US Letter (8.5×11 in)
- Use: attach or insert in the tenant email

### Poster (print shop)
- Files: `V#/GreenwayStreetEats_Poster_18x24_BLEED_V#.pdf`
- **Trim size: 18×24 in** (this is the finished poster)
- File includes **0.375 in bleed** on each side (full sheet **18.75×24.75 in**) plus **crop / cut marks** at the trim
- Use: send this PDF to the printer; tell them trim is 18×24 with bleed and crop marks included
- Do **not** use the rolling MP4 animations on the printed poster

### Rolling truck clips (web / review only)
- Files: `animations/truck-roll-v#.mp4` (also in the animations zip)
- Use: website, review page, social / motion mockups
- **Not** for print
